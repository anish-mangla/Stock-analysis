INTRADAY DEEP DIVE — FINDINGS SUMMARY
======================================

Data: 53M 1-minute bars, 100 tickers, 2020-2025, split-adjusted
Baseline: +102.5% (close entry, +5%/60d, fixed 1/10, no look-ahead biases)


BLOCK A: ENTRY TIMING (v01-v07)
================================

V01 — Entry day is basically flat (open to close = -0.06%). No big consistent dip.
      Lowest point is 2:27 PM at -0.13%. Winners drift up, losers drift down.

V02 — Best entry time: 2:00 PM (+114.3% vs +102.5% at close = +11.8% improvement)
      Morning entries (9:35-10:30) are worst at ~87%. Afternoon consistently better.

V03 — VWAP entry (+80.3%) is WORSE than close. 2:00 PM price (+109.5%) confirms V02.

V04 — LIMIT ORDER at open-2% gives +120.3% (+17.8% vs close). Fills 34% of time.
      When it fills, you get a 2% discount. When it doesn't, you buy at close.

V06 — *** BEST ENTRY STRATEGY ***
      Wait for 0.5% dip from open, then buy. Fallback to close if no dip.
      Result: +162.3% (+58% improvement over close baseline!)
      Dip happens 69% of the time. Higher dip thresholds also work but less often.

V07 — Entry delay: 1-day (+104%) and 2-day (+102.5%) are tied. 0-day worst.


BLOCK B: EXIT TIMING (v08)
============================

V08 — Winners overshoot target by +1.87% on average (median +0.96%).
      Target hit at 10:56 AM on average, 54% hit in first hour.
      50% of winners close above target, 50% below.
      → A trailing stop could capture ~1% extra on winners.


BLOCK C: STOP LOSS (v16)
==========================

V16 — Entry day drawdown: winners -2.02%, losers -2.44%. Difference too small
      for a reliable intraday stop loss signal.


BLOCK D: VOLUME SIGNALS (v17-v20)
===================================

V17 — Drop day volume is 1.65x normal on average. No strong WR signal from
      drop-day volume (83% at low vol vs 78% at extreme vol — small spread).

V19 — *** STRONG SIGNAL ***
      Entry day relative volume predicts success:
        Quiet (<0.8x): 90.6% WR
        Normal (0.8-1.2x): 83.1% WR
        Active (1.2-2x): 76.3% WR
        Surge (2x+): 63.3% WR
      → If entry day is quiet, the selling is exhausted. If volume surges, avoid.

V20 — Higher closing auction volume on drop day = better bounce (86% for 20%+
      vs 77% for <5%). Heavy closing volume = capitulation = good sign.


BLOCK E: PATTERN RECOGNITION (v25)
=====================================

V25 — *** STRONGEST SIGNAL FOUND ***
      Overnight gap after drop predicts success:
        T+1 gap up 3%+: 97.5% WR (527 events)
        T+1 gap up 1-3%: 91.9% WR (1,357 events)
        T+1 gap down 3%+: 61.1% WR (517 events)
      
      T+2 gap (our entry day):
        Gap up 1%+: 95.0% WR (2,832 events)
        Gap down 1%+: 65.4% WR (2,451 events)
      
      → 30 percentage point spread. This is the single best predictor.


BLOCK F: NEWS × INTRADAY (v26-v33)
=====================================

V26 — Different event types have different intraday shapes:
        guidance_cut: rallies all day (+0.22% by close)
        earnings_miss: drops -0.56% by 11:30 then recovers
        regulatory_legal: rallies all day (+1.00% by close)
        competitive_threat: pops early then fades

V27 — Optimal entry time varies by event type:
        earnings_miss: buy at 11:30 (saves 0.43%)
        guidance_cut: buy at 10:00 (saves 0.31%)
        regulatory_legal: buy at 10:30 (saves 1.01%)

V29 — High stress days: morning pop (+0.27% at 10:30) then fade to -0.18%.
      Calm days: basically flat all day.

V31 — No-catalyst drops (80% WR) are flat all day. Catalyst drops (75% WR)
      show more intraday volatility.

============================================

   This alone could transform the system.
